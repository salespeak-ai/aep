## Trademark

No trademark is claimed over the name "AEP", over "Agent Entry Point", or over
the mark artwork. Anyone may use them, including direct competitors of the
authors, without permission, attribution, or notice.

This is deliberate. A mark whose use can be withheld by one company is that
company's badge, not a shared convention.

## On the usage rules in SPEC.md

SPEC.md lists things you should not do to the mark: place a logo inside it,
recolor it into a brand mark, animate it, alter its proportions.

Those describe conformity to the AEP specification. They are not license
conditions. CC0 imposes none, and no trademark backs them, so they are
unenforceable by design. A mark that stops being recognizable stops being
useful, which is the only reason to follow them.
